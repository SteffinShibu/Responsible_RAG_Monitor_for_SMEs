## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy outlines the rules, conditions, and timelines governing product refunds, exchanges, and replacements for purchases made with BrightPath Office Supplies. It is designed to ensure compliance with the Dutch Consumer Protection Law (Consumentenrecht), the EU Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU), and fair commercial standards for business-to-business (B2B) accounts.

---

## 2. General Return Windows
Return windows differ significantly based on whether the customer is classified as a Business-to-Consumer (B2C) retail buyer or a Business-to-Business (B2B) commercial client.

### 2.1 B2C Retail Customers
* **Right of Withdrawal (Herroepingsrecht):** Under EU law, B2C customers have a standard return window of **14 calendar days** from the day they (or a designated third party) receive physical possession of the goods.
* **No Justification Needed:** The customer does not need to provide a reason for return within this 14-day window.
* **Condition of Goods:** Goods must be returned in their original packaging, unused, and in a re-sellable condition. If the item has been assembled (e.g., an office chair) or used excessively beyond what is necessary to evaluate its nature, BrightPath reserves the right to deduct up to **50% of the refund value** for depreciation.

### 2.2 B2B Commercial Customers
* **Standard Return Window:** B2B customers are granted a voluntary return window of **7 business days** from delivery.
* **Restocking Fee:** All B2B returns that are not due to defect or error on BrightPath’s part are subject to a **15% restocking fee**, calculated based on the net invoice value of the returned products.
* **Custom/Bulk Orders:** Customized items (e.g., branded stationery, custom-sized executive desks) and bulk orders exceeding **€1,500.00** are non-returnable and non-refundable unless verified as defective upon arrival.

## 3. Replacement Rules for Defective or Wrong Items
If an item is received in a damaged, defective, or incorrect state, BrightPath will resolve the issue at no additional cost to the customer.

### 3.1 Damaged Upon Arrival
* **Reporting Timeline:** Customers must report transit damage within **48 hours of delivery**.
* **Evidence Required:** Clear digital photographs of the outer packaging, inner packaging, and the damaged item itself must be submitted via email or the customer portal.
* **Resolution:** Once verified by a human support agent, BrightPath will dispatch a replacement item within 1 business day via express courier. A pre-paid return shipping label will be provided to retrieve the damaged item.

### 3.2 Wrong Item Sent
* **Reporting Timeline:** Must be reported within **14 calendar days** for B2C and **5 business days** for B2B.
* **Resolution:** Upon verification of the picking error, a replacement order is initiated. The incorrect item must be returned unused in its original packaging.

---

## 4. Late Delivery Compensation & Vouchers
BrightPath aims for on-time delivery. However, when delays occur due to courier failures (PostNL, DHL, or DPD), the following goodwill thresholds apply:

* **Delays exceeding 3 business days (beyond estimated date):** A formal apology and a **€10.00 digital discount voucher** for their next purchase (minimum spend €50.00).
* **Delays exceeding 7 business days:** The customer can choose between canceling the order for a full refund or accepting a **€25.00 digital discount voucher**.
* *Exceptions:* Delays due to customer error (e.g., incorrect delivery address, missed delivery attempts, lack of access to office buildings) are not eligible for compensation.

## 5. High-Value Order Restrictions
Orders with a total value exceeding **€500.00 (inclusive of VAT)** are designated as "High-Value Transactions." 

* **No Automated Approvals:** Under no circumstances is the AI assistant permitted to authorize refunds, replacements, or issue credit notes for High-Value Transactions.
* **Mandatory Dual-Sign Off:** Any refund or replacement for an order > €500.00 requires physical or digital sign-off from both:
  1. A Customer Support Supervisor, and
  2. The Finance Department / Accountant.
* **Escalation Path:** Inquiries concerning refunds for High-Value Transactions must be immediately routed to a senior human agent and tagged with `#HIGH-VALUE-REFUND`.

---

## 6. Scenarios Where the AI Must NOT Promise a Refund
To maintain financial control and prevent unauthorized commitments, the AI Assistant must use defensive phrasing and never confirm a refund is "approved" or "processed" when:

1. **The order status is "In Transit" or "With Courier":** A refund cannot be processed until the courier returns the package to our warehouse or provides official written confirmation that the shipment is declared lost.
2. **The return request falls outside the permitted windows:** (i.e., > 14 days for B2C, > 7 days for B2B).
3. **The item in question is a Customized Product or Software License:** Software licenses with revealed keys, activated printer toner cartridges, or custom-engraved items are completely excluded from return.
4. **The customer claims they "never received the package" but the tracking shows "Delivered with Signature":** This is considered a tracking conflict and must be escalated to a human team member for investigation with the shipping courier (see **BP-SOP-DTS-003**).
