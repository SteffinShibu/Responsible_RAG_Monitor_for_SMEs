## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy defines the operational standards, communication channels, response time targets, and compliance requirements for customer support operations at BrightPath Office Supplies. It specifically governs how both human agents and artificial intelligence (AI) support assistants interact with customers, resolve issues, and handle sensitive scenarios in both business-to-consumer (B2C) and business-to-business (B2B) transactions.

As a Small-to-Medium Enterprise (SME) based in the Netherlands, BrightPath Office Supplies operates under the Dutch Civil Code (Burgerlijk Wetboek) and EU consumer protection regulations. This document works in conjunction with our **Refund and Replacement Policy (BP-POL-RRP-002)**, **Delivery and Tracking SOP (BP-SOP-DTS-003)**, and **Escalation Matrix (BP-MX-EM-005)**.

## 2. Customer Support Channels

1. **Email Support:** `support@brightpathofficesupplies.nl` (Monitored 08:30 to 17:30 CET, Monday through Friday).
2. **Web Chat / AI Assistant:** Available 24/7 on the customer portal. Managed by the "BrightPath AI Assistant" with automatic routing to human agents during business hours.

## 4. AI Assistant Scope of Authority
The BrightPath AI Assistant is an automated tool designed to answer routine, informational queries. It operates under strict technical boundaries to prevent unauthorized financial commitments or inaccurate policy interpretations.

### 4.1 What the AI Assistant CAN Answer
* **Inquiry Status:** Tracking shipment status, provided the customer enters a valid Order ID (e.g., BP-2024-12345) and matching delivery zip code.
* **Basic Policies:** Summarizing refund windows, replacement criteria, and delivery zones based strictly on the approved knowledge base documents.
* **Product Specifications:** Providing information on dimensions, materials, and features of items in our catalog (e.g., ergonomic chairs, whiteboard sizes, cartridge compatibility).
* **Hours of Operation:** Confirming office and telephone support operating hours.

### 4.2 What the AI Assistant CANNOT Answer / Must Decline
* **Direct Order Modification:** The AI cannot modify order details, delivery addresses, or items in a processing queue. Customers must be directed to a human agent.
* **Financial Approvals:** The AI is strictly prohibited from promising, calculating, or approving partial refunds, goodwill compensation, or delivery delay vouchers.
* **Complex Disputes:** The AI must not mediate disputes regarding defective merchandise or contract pricing.
* **Legal Liability:** The AI must never admit liability on behalf of BrightPath Office Supplies or interpret terms of contract outside of direct copy-paste templates from our legal terms.

## 5. Mandated Human Review & Escalation Triggers
The AI assistant must trigger a transfer to a human support agent (or create a high-priority ticket if outside business hours) in the following circumstances:

1. **Negative Sentiment / Frustration:** Detection of profanity, repeated exclamation marks, or phrases such as "unacceptable," "terrible service," "reporting you to ACM" (Authority for Consumers and Markets), or "demand to speak to a person."
2. **Third-Time Failure:** If the AI assistant has provided two answers and the user still indicates the question is not resolved (e.g., clicking "No" on "Was this helpful?" twice).
3. **High-Value Customer Transactions:** Any inquiry regarding an order with a total transaction value exceeding **€500.00** must be escalated when a complaint is registered.
4. **GDPR / Privacy Inquiries:** Any request involving data deletion, subject access requests (SAR), or complaints about privacy and data usage.
5. **Ambiguous or Contradictory Scenarios:** When the user query describes a scenario that overlaps multiple policies with conflicting outcomes (e.g., a delivery delayed past the 14-day return window where the item is also customized).

## 6. Tone of Voice & Style Guidelines
To maintain our brand image as a reliable, supportive partner for Dutch and European businesses, all communications—human or AI-generated—must adhere to these tone-of-voice rules:

* **Professional and Respectful:** Use formal yet warm language. In Dutch, default to the polite "u" form for business partners and older demographics, but use "je" if the customer's initial query uses informal language or if addressing individual retail consumers in a casual setting. The AI Assistant should default to a polite, neutral, yet accessible Dutch or English depending on the prompt.
* **Empathetic and Active:** Acknowledge frustrations immediately. Example: *"I understand that a delayed delivery can disrupt your workplace. Let me look into this right away for you."*
* **Clear and Direct:** Avoid overly complex corporate jargon. Do not use speculative language.
* **Humble and Transparent:** If the AI is answering, it must identify itself clearly. *“Hello! I am the BrightPath Virtual Assistant. I can help with delivery tracking, policy questions, and product specifications. For complex matters, I will loop in one of my human colleagues.”*

## 7. Prohibited Claims and Hallucination Mitigation
To protect BrightPath from legal liability and compliance violations under the EU AI Act and national advertising laws, the following constraints must be programmed into all prompt instructions and monitored by quality assurance:

1. **No "Guaranteed" Delivery Dates:** Neither agents nor the AI may guarantee a specific delivery hour or day unless a formal "Guaranteed Express Courier" service has been paid for and confirmed. The standard phrase is *"Our delivery partner typically delivers within 1 to 2 business days, but this is an estimate."*
2. **No Custom Discount Creation:** The AI must not generate or promise discount codes or promotional prices that are not actively stored in the active system catalog.
3. **No Safety Warranties Beyond Manufacturer Specs:** Agents and AI must not claim an ergonomic chair "cures back pain" or "prevents injury." Only manufacturer-specified structural limits (e.g., "certified for up to 120kg weight capacity") may be quoted.
4. **Strict "Source Citation" Requirement:** If the AI system answers a policy question, it must output a brief parenthetical source reference to this document pack (e.g., *"[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 2.1]"*). If no clear source exists, the AI must state: *"I cannot find an exact policy rule to answer your query. Let me connect you to a human support agent."*

## 8. Handling Unclear Policy Questions
When a customer scenario does not neatly fit the written policies, or if there is an apparent contradiction:
* The AI assistant must not guess. It must state its limitations and offer to route the chat.
* Human agents must consult their direct Shift Supervisor if an ambiguity involves a transaction above **€150.00**.
* If a customer points out an inconsistency in our documentation (e.g., a discrepancy between the FAQ and the official terms of service), the ticket must be flagged with the tag `#DOC-DISCREPANCY` and routed to the Head of CX for resolution within 5 business days.
