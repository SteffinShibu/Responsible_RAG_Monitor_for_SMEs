## 1. Purpose & Scope
This Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) document provides clear, structured answers to common customer and employee inquiries. It is designed to be parsed easily by Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines and used directly as the reference knowledge source for the "BrightPath AI Assistant." 

All answers are derived directly from our primary policy documents: **Customer Support Policy (BP-POL-CSP-001)**, **Refund and Replacement Policy (BP-POL-RRP-002)**, **Delivery and Tracking SOP (BP-SOP-DTS-003)**, and **Data Privacy and AI Usage Policy (BP-POL-DPA-004)**.

## 2. Customer FAQs

### 2.1 Shipping & Delivery

#### Q1: How long does standard delivery take within the Netherlands?
**A:** Standard delivery within the Netherlands takes **1 to 2 business days**. Orders placed before **16:00 CET** on business days are dispatched the same day. *[Source: BP-SOP-DTS-003, Section 2.1]*

#### Q2: Do you ship orders to Belgium and Germany? How long does it take?
**A:** Yes, we ship to Belgium (1 to 2 business days) and Germany (2 to 4 business days). *[Source: BP-SOP-DTS-003, Section 2.1]*

#### Q3: How much does standard shipping cost?
**A:** Standard shipping is free for orders over **€50.00**. For orders below this amount, a flat rate of **€4.95** applies to Netherlands addresses. For other EU countries, please check our checkout shipping calculator.

#### Q4: My order was supposed to arrive yesterday, but tracking hasn't updated. What should I do?
**A:** Please provide your **Order ID** and **delivery zip code** to our chat interface. If your package has been delayed by more than 3 business days, you are eligible for a **€10.00 goodwill digital discount voucher**. *[Source: BP-SOP-DTS-003, Section 3.1 & BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 4]*

#### Q5: Tracking says my order is "Delivered," but I don't have it. Can you refund me?
**A:** We cannot issue an immediate refund when tracking states "Delivered." We ask that you first check with reception, close neighbors, or local pickup points. If it remains missing, we must open a formal inquiry with the carrier, which takes 3 to 10 business days. *[Source: BP-SOP-DTS-003, Section 4.1]*

#### Q6: Can I change my delivery address after my order is placed?
**A:** Our AI Assistant cannot process delivery changes. Please let me connect you with a live customer support agent during business hours (08:30–17:30 CET) to see if we can catch the order in our Almere warehouse before dispatch. *[Source: BP-POL-CSP-001, Section 4.2]*

#### Q7: I received a message asking for customs fees for my delivery. Is this correct?
**A:** BrightPath ships all orders from Almere, Netherlands. We do not ship from outside the EU Schengen zone, so you should not receive customs bills. This could indicate a phishing scam or carrier sorting error. Let me escalate this immediately to our supervisor team. *[Source: BP-SOP-DTS-003, Section 7]*

#### Q8: My package arrived wet and crushed. What is the process for a replacement?
**A:** We apologize for the damage. Please report this to us within **48 hours of delivery** and provide digital photographs of the damaged item and packaging. Once verified by a team member, we will dispatch a replacement within 1 business day. *[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 3.1]*

#### Q9: What happens if a shipment is officially declared "Lost"?
**A:** A shipment is declared "Lost" if there are no tracking updates for over 10 consecutive days within the Benelux, or 15 days for international EU shipments. We will then process a priority replacement or full refund according to your choice. *[Source: BP-SOP-DTS-003, Section 4.3]*

#### Q10: What does the status "Returned to Sender" mean?
**A:** This status indicates a delivery failure (such as an incorrect address, or repeated missed deliveries). I cannot process address changes directly; I will route your chat history to a live agent to arrange a re-delivery. *[Source: BP-SOP-DTS-003, Section 7]*

### 2.2 Refunds & Replacements

#### Q11: How many days do I have to return an item if I change my mind?
**A:** If you are a B2C retail consumer, you have **14 calendar days** from delivery to return items under the EU Right of Withdrawal. If you are a B2B business customer, the voluntary return window is **7 business days**. *[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 2.1 & 2.2]*

#### Q12: Are there restocking fees for returning commercial B2B orders?
**A:** Yes. All voluntary B2B returns are subject to a **15% restocking fee** calculated on the net invoice value of the items. *[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 2.2]*

#### Q13: Can I return a customized whiteboard or desk featuring my company logo?
**A:** No. Customized, branded items and bulk orders exceeding **€1,500.00** are non-returnable and non-refundable unless they arrive defective. *[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 2.2]*

#### Q14: I bought an office chair, assembled it, and sat on it, but it’s uncomfortable. Can I return it?
**A:** Yes, but if an item has been fully assembled or used excessively, we reserve the right to deduct up to **50% of the refund value** due to depreciation. *[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 2.1]*

#### Q15: Can I get a refund for a printer toner cartridge that I have opened and tested?
**A:** No. Opened software licenses, activated digital keys, and opened printer toner cartridges are strictly excluded from our return policy for health, safety, and operational reasons. *[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 6]*

#### Q16: My order value is €650.00, and I want to return the whole shipment. Can you process this refund now?
**A:** Because this transaction exceeds our high-value threshold of **€500.00**, our automated systems cannot process this refund. I will transfer your request to a Senior Support Supervisor and the Finance Department for dual-sign off. *[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 5]*

#### Q17: Do I have to pay for return shipping?
**A:** For standard change-of-mind returns, the customer covers return shipping. For defective, incorrect, or transit-damaged items, we provide a pre-paid return label. *[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 2.1, 2.2, & 3.1]*

#### Q18: Can the AI Assistant directly authorize or schedule my refund?
**A:** No. The AI Assistant is prohibited from directly authorizing or processing refunds, partial refunds, or financial credits. All transactions are routed to our secure accounting workflow for human review. *[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 6]*

#### Q19: How long does it take for a refund to appear in my bank account?
**A:** Once we receive and verify the returned item in our Almere warehouse, we process the refund within **3 to 5 business days**. Payment will return via the original payment method used.

#### Q20: What happens if my delivery is more than 7 business days late?
**A:** You can choose to cancel the order for a full refund or accept the package along with a **€25.00 goodwill digital discount voucher**. *[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 4]*

### 2.3 General Customer Support

#### Q21: What are your customer support telephone hours?
**A:** You can call our support team at **+31 (0) 20 555 0199** between **09:00 and 17:00 CET**, Monday through Friday. *[Source: BP-POL-CSP-001, Section 2]*

#### Q22: Can I get a discount if I purchase 10 ergonomic office chairs?
**A:** I cannot generate custom discounts or negotiate prices. I will transfer your inquiry to our B2B Sales Department during business hours to prepare a customized quotation. *[Source: BP-POL-CSP-001, Section 7]*

#### Q23: Is the AI Assistant's advice legally binding?
**A:** No. The AI Assistant provides informational answers based on company policies. It cannot make custom policy exemptions or establish contract liabilities. *[Source: BP-POL-CSP-001, Section 4.2]*

#### Q24: How can I register a formal complaint about my customer experience?
**A:** We are sorry to hear you've had a bad experience. Please email `support@brightpathofficesupplies.nl` with the word "COMPLAINT" in the subject, or ask me to transfer you to a supervisor. Level 1 complaints are resolved within 4 hours; Level 2 and 3 within 2 business days. *[Source: BP-SOP-CCH-008, Section 3]*

#### Q25: I am extremely unhappy with this automated system. I want to speak to a person!
**A:** I understand your frustration. I am transferring you to our live human support queue immediately. One of our agents will be with you shortly. *[Source: BP-POL-DPA-004, Section 5]*

### 2.4 Privacy & Data Security

#### Q26: Is my personal information secure with BrightPath Office Supplies?
**A:** Yes. We comply strictly with the EU GDPR (AVG). Customer data is kept only for specific, lawful fulfillment actions and is stored on secure European servers. *[Source: BP-POL-DPA-004, Section 1 & 2]*

#### Q27: How do I request the deletion of all my personal data from your systems?
**A:** Under the GDPR "Right to be Forgotten," you can request complete deletion of your personal data. Please submit a request, and I will route this to our Data Protection Officer (DPO) for compliance review within 72 hours. *[Source: BP-MX-EM-005, Section 4]*

#### Q28: How long do you store our support chat logs?
**A:** Support chat logs are retained on secure cloud instances for exactly **90 days** and are then automatically purged. *[Source: BP-POL-DPA-004, Section 2]*

#### Q29: Can I give you my Citizen Service Number (BSN) or credit card number over chat?
**A:** No. We never require your BSN, and credit card numbers must never be entered in chat logs. All payments are completed through our secure third-party provider, Mollie. *[Source: BP-POL-DPA-004, Section 3.2]*

#### Q30: Do your AI tools use my chat prompts to train public machine learning models?
**A:** No. Our internal AI Assistant and monitoring tools run on secured enterprise instances with zero-data-retention commitments for model retraining. *[Source: BP-POL-DPA-004, Section 4.2]*

## 3. Internal Employee FAQs

### 3.1 AI Tool Usage

#### Q31: Can I paste code from our webshop ERP system into ChatGPT to debug it?
**A:** No. Employees are strictly prohibited from pasting proprietary code, API keys, or system credentials into public, non-enterprise AI systems. *[Source: BP-POL-DPA-004, Section 4.2]*

#### Q32: Am I allowed to use the internal BrightPath AI Copilot to draft a response to a complex customer?
**A:** Yes. You may use our secured internal copilot to draft responses, but you must manually verify and edit the output before sending. You are personally responsible for the accuracy of the email. *[Source: BP-POL-AIU-006, Section 3 & 6]*

#### Q33: What is the disciplinary penalty for uploading a spreadsheet of customer addresses to an external AI tool?
**A:** This represents a major GDPR data breach and a direct policy violation. It will result in an immediate HR investigation and disciplinary action, up to and including termination for gross misconduct. *[Source: BP-POL-AIU-006, Section 6]*

#### Q34: What is the "Human Owner" rule regarding AI use?
**A:** If an employee publishes an error, issues an incorrect credit, or violates a policy based on AI recommendations, the employee is held fully responsible, not the AI tool. *[Source: BP-POL-AIU-006, Section 6]*

#### Q35: Can we use an AI tool to automatically filter and reject job applicants?
**A:** No. Under the EU AI Act, recruitment tools are classified as high risk. Human-in-the-loop review is mandatory, and automated filtering of resumes is strictly prohibited. *[Source: BP-POL-AIU-006, Section 3]*

### 3.2 Escalation & Support Operations

#### Q36: What is the maximum refund a Tier-2 Senior Support Agent can approve?
**A:** Tier-2 Senior Support Agents can approve refunds and credit notes up to **€150.00**. *[Source: BP-MX-EM-005, Section 3.2]*

#### Q37: Who must approve a refund for a damaged office desk valued at €575.00?
**A:** Any refund exceeding **€500.00** represents a High-Value Transaction, which requires written approval from both a Customer Support Supervisor and the Finance Department. *[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 5]*

#### Q38: When does a delayed package trigger an investigation with PostNL?
**A:** If a package has not arrived within **3 business days** past the estimated window, and is not located after a GPS check, we must initiate a formal dispute with PostNL within 5 business days of the marked delivery date. *[Source: BP-SOP-DTS-003, Section 4.1 & 4.2]*

#### Q39: What should I do if a customer threatens to report BrightPath to ACM or take legal action?
**A:** Flag the interaction with `#LEGAL-THREAT` and transfer the customer immediately to the Tier-4 Executive Queue (Head of CX / COO). *[Source: BP-MX-EM-005, Section 4]*

#### Q40: What happens if the AI assistant's RAGAS confidence score drops below 0.70?
**A:** The system will automatically block the automated output, show a standard fallback transfer message, and route the customer to a Tier-2 Support Agent. *[Source: BP-MX-EM-005, Section 4]*

### 3.3 Logistics & Warehouse

#### Q41: Where is our main fulfillment center located?
**A:** All shipments are processed and dispatched from our central warehouse in **Almere, Netherlands**. *[Source: BP-SOP-DTS-003, Section 2]*

#### Q42: What is the weekday shipping cut-off time for same-day dispatch?
**A:** Orders must be placed before **16:00 CET** on business days to be dispatched on the same day. *[Source: BP-SOP-DTS-003, Section 2.1]*

#### Q43: How long do we keep customer support chat logs before deletion?
**A:** To comply with our data minimization policy, chat logs are archived for **90 days** and then automatically purged. *[Source: BP-POL-DPA-004, Section 2]*

#### Q44: What do I do if a customer reports that their B2B order has incorrect items, but they report it 10 business days after delivery?
**A:** The B2B return window for wrong/defective items is **5 business days**. Because this report is late, do not approve automated dispatch. Escalate this ticket to the Customer Support Supervisor for review. *[Source: BP-POL-RRP-002, Section 3.2]*

#### Q45: How long does a shipping carrier typically take to resolve a formal delivery investigation?
**A:** PostNL, DHL, and DPD typically resolve standard investigations within **3 to 10 business days**. *[Source: BP-SOP-DTS-003, Section 4.1]*

### 3.4 Security & Vendor Risk

#### Q46: Can we sign a 3-year contract with a new cloud storage vendor containing our customer databases?
**A:** No. Critical Risk software vendors must have contracts limited to a maximum initial term of 12 months with 6-month reviews, to prevent vendor lock-in. *[Source: BP-CHK-VRC-007, Section 4]*

#### Q47: What geographic requirement applies to vendors processing BrightPath customer data?
**A:** All customer data must reside within the European Economic Area (EEA) or in jurisdictions with a formal EU adequacy decision. *[Source: BP-CHK-VRC-007, Section 3.1]*

#### Q48: What is the maximum acceptable data breach notification window from a third-party vendor?
**A:** Critical and High Risk vendors must guarantee written notification of any suspected or confirmed data breach within **24 hours** of discovery. *[Source: BP-CHK-VRC-007, Section 3.2]*

#### Q49: What is the formal tag used for a ticket when a data breach is suspected?
**A:** Suspected data breaches must be logged with the tag `#DATA-BREACH` and escalated to the Data Protection Officer immediately. *[Source: BP-POL-DPA-004, Section 7]*

#### Q50: Can we buy software from an AI vendor whose models learn from our input prompts?
**A:** No. Our vendor security policies require that any AI vendor provide zero data retention (ZDR) terms for model training. *[Source: BP-CHK-VRC-007, Section 3.3 & 5]*
