# Human Escalation Checklist

Use this checklist when the AI assistant flags a query for escalation, or when a human agent reviews an escalated case.

## Before Accepting Escalation

- [ ] Confirm the escalation reason is clearly documented
- [ ] Review the original customer query
- [ ] Check which policy documents are relevant
- [ ] Verify the AI assistant's answer for accuracy
- [ ] Check if the same issue has been escalated before

## While Handling the Case

- [ ] Follow the relevant SOP for the issue type
- [ ] Do not rely solely on the AI assistant's recommendation
- [ ] Double-check refund/compensation amounts against policy
- [ ] Verify customer identity if PII is involved
- [ ] Log the outcome in the incident log

## Escalation Categories

| Category | Required Action | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| High-value transaction (>€500) | Supervisor + Finance dual sign-off | Customer Support Supervisor |
| Legal threat or lawsuit | Forward to Legal team | Legal Counsel |
| Privacy/GDPR complaint | Forward to Data Protection Officer | DPO |
| AI-related complaint | Log in AI incident register | AI Governance Lead |
| Ambiguous policy | Research + document interpretation | Policy Owner |
| Custom pricing/discount | Forward to Sales Manager | Sales Manager |

## After Resolution

- [ ] Update the incident log
- [ ] Flag if system prompt or policy needs updating
- [ ] Provide feedback to the AI team on edge cases encountered
